# Ride Angels — Privacy (SMS / authentication)

**Last updated:** 2026-08-28

Ride Angels is operated by **Hyperion App Studio** (sole proprietor: Devin Cooper).

## What we collect for phone sign-in

- Mobile phone number you enter to create an account or sign in
- One-time passcodes sent by SMS for authentication
- Basic account profile data you provide in the app (name, email if added, etc.)

## How we use it

- Send transactional SMS one-time codes so you can sign in securely
- Operate the Ride Angels service (rides, trusted circle, notifications)
- We do **not** sell your phone number
- We do **not** use SMS for marketing or advertising

## SMS specifics

- Message frequency: only when you request a sign-in or verification code
- Message and data rates may apply
- Reply **STOP** to opt out of SMS; reply **HELP** for help
- Carriers are not liable for delayed or undelivered messages

## Sharing

Phone numbers and auth data are processed by our infrastructure providers
(including Supabase Auth and Twilio) solely to deliver authentication SMS and
run the app backend.
