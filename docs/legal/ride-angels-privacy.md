# Ride Angels — Privacy Policy

**Last updated:** 2026-09-02

Ride Angels is operated by **Hyperion App Studio** (sole proprietor: Devin Cooper).

This policy describes how we collect, use, and share information when you use the Ride Angels iOS and Android apps (`org.rideangels.app`) and related services.

## What we collect

**Account and profile**

- Phone number and/or email address you use to create an account or sign in
- One-time passcodes sent by SMS or email for authentication
- Name and other profile details you enter
- Optional profile photo (camera or photo library)

**Ride and circle content**

- Appointment and ride details you create (times, pickup/destination labels, notes)
- Ride offers, assignments, and status updates (including On my way)
- Trusted-circle invites and relationships (email and/or phone identifiers you enter to invite someone)
- In-app notifications and optional Feedback & ideas posts (including screenshots you attach)

**Device and notifications**

- Push notification tokens (Apple Push Notification service / Firebase Cloud Messaging) when you enable push
- Basic technical data needed to run the service (for example account IDs stored in our database)

**What we do not collect**

- We do **not** track your live GPS location or sell location data
- We do **not** use advertising or tracking SDKs
- Device calendar events created for claimed rides are written with the operating system calendar APIs and are **not** uploaded to Ride Angels servers as a calendar dump
- We do **not** send marketing SMS. SMS is for authentication one-time codes only

## How we use information

- Authenticate you and secure your account
- Operate rides, trusted circles, invites, reminders, and in-app / push notifications
- Let you Call or Text another person using your device’s phone or Messages apps (we open those apps with the number on the profile — we do not place the call ourselves)
- Improve reliability and support (including responding to email you send us)
- Comply with law where required

We do **not** sell your personal information. We do **not** use your phone number or email for advertising.

## SMS specifics (phone sign-in)

- Message frequency: only when you request a sign-in or verification code
- Message and data rates may apply
- Reply **STOP** to opt out of SMS; reply **HELP** for help
- Carriers are not liable for delayed or undelivered messages
- See also: https://hyperionappstudio.com/rideangels/sms-opt-in/

## Sharing

We share data with infrastructure providers **only** to run the service, for example:

- **Supabase** — authentication, database, and backend
- **Twilio** — phone OTP SMS (Twilio Verify)
- **Resend** — transactional email for circle invites (when you invite by email)
- **Apple / Google** — delivery of push notifications when you enable them

We do not sell personal information to data brokers or advertisers.

## Retention and deletion

We keep account and ride data while your account is active. You can delete your account in the app: **Profile → Account & Security → Delete account**. Deletion removes your account access and associated personal data from our active systems, subject to limited backups and legal retention. You can also email support@hyperionappstudio.com to request help with deletion or a copy of your information.

## Children

Ride Angels is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

## Changes

We may update this policy. The “Last updated” date above will change when we do. Continued use of the app after an update means you accept the revised policy.
